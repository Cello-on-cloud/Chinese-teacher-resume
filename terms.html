The agreement 协议说明

These terms apply when you book or pay for a lesson with Zhixia Chinese (“I”, “me”, “my”). By booking a lesson or completing payment, you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not book.

These terms sit alongside the information on the pricing page and the privacy policy. If something in a written message from me differs from these terms, the written message applies to that booking.

Lessons 课程安排

  • Lessons are one-to-one, online, and normally 55 minutes long.
  • We meet on Zoom or Google Meet unless we agree otherwise in writing.
  • I prepare materials based on your level, goals and interests. You do not need to buy a textbook unless we agree on one.
  • Lesson times are agreed in your time zone. Please double-check the time when you book, especially around daylight-saving changes.
  • If you arrive late, the lesson still ends at the scheduled time. If I arrive late, I will make up the lost time or adjust the fee.

Booking & payment 预约与付款

  • Lessons are confirmed only after payment has been received or a payment arrangement has been agreed in writing.
  • Prices are shown in US dollars. Any applicable taxes, bank fees or currency-conversion charges are explained at checkout or by your payment provider.
  • Single lessons, 10-lesson packs and 20-lesson packs are personal to the learner and cannot be transferred or resold.
  • Unless we agree otherwise, lesson packages are valid for six months from the date of purchase.
  • I may change prices for future bookings, but a price already paid for a confirmed package will be honoured.

Rescheduling 改期规则

  • You can reschedule at no cost with at least 24 hours’ notice before the lesson.
  • With less than 24 hours’ notice, the lesson counts as used. I will still try to be flexible if there is an emergency.
  • If you do not join within 15 minutes of the start time and have not contacted me, the lesson is treated as a no-show and counts as used.
  • Rescheduled lessons must take place within the validity period of your package.

Refunds & fit guarantee 退款与适配保证

If after your first lesson you feel we are not the right fit, email me within seven days of that lesson. I will refund the cost of all remaining unused lessons in your package. The first lesson itself is non-refundable.

  • Refunds are returned to the original payment method. Your provider may take several business days to show the credit.
  • I cover the payment processing fee on refunds I initiate under this guarantee.
  • Used lessons, no-shows and lessons missed with less than 24 hours’ notice are not refundable.
  • Nothing in these terms limits any mandatory consumer rights you have under the law that applies to you.

Your responsibilities 学员责任

  • Join from a reasonably quiet place with a stable internet connection, a working microphone and, where possible, a camera.
  • Treat the lesson as a shared commitment: arrive on time, engage respectfully and tell me if something is not working for you.
  • Do not share your lesson link or account with anyone else.
  • If the learner is under 18, a parent or guardian must book the lessons and remain responsible for the learner’s participation and safety.

Teacher cancellation 老师取消课程

If I need to cancel or move a lesson, I will give you as much notice as possible and offer a replacement time. If we cannot find a suitable time, you may choose a refund or credit for that lesson. I will not charge you for a lesson I cannot teach.

Conduct & safety 课堂行为与安全

  • Lessons should be a respectful, harassment-free space. I may end a lesson if someone is threatening, abusive or behaving inappropriately.
  • Please do not record a lesson without asking first. If we agree to a recording, it is for personal study only and must not be published or shared.
  • For online safety, I do not ask for passwords, full card numbers or identity documents by email.
  • Lessons are educational, not emergency, medical, legal or mental-health support.

Materials & copyright 教材与版权

Lesson plans, slides, exercises and other materials I create remain my intellectual property. You receive a personal, non-transferable licence to use them for your own learning. Please do not resell, republish or distribute them. Materials you share with me remain yours; I will use them only to prepare and deliver your lessons.

Results & liability 学习成果与责任

I will teach with reasonable care and skill, but language learning depends on many factors, including practice outside class. I cannot guarantee a particular exam score, fluency level or timeline.

To the extent permitted by law, my total liability arising from the lessons is limited to the amount you paid for the affected lessons. I am not liable for indirect or consequential losses, or for failures caused by your internet connection, device or third-party platforms. Nothing here excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded.

Privacy 隐私

I handle personal information as described in the privacy policy. That policy explains what I collect, why, how long I keep it and how to exercise your rights.
