About your teacher

A patient teacher who listens first.

先听懂你，再陪你开口。

Hi, I’m Lin Zhixia (林知夏) — though most students call me Xiàxia. I’ve spent the last six years teaching Chinese to adults, teenagers and heritage learners from more than thirty countries, mostly one-to-one and always online.

My lessons are calm, structured and conversational. We use real situations — ordering tea, writing an email, preparing for HSK, talking with family — so the language becomes something you can use, not just study. And yes, we will laugh when the tones go sideways.

“You don’t need to be naturally good at languages. You need a clear next step, a patient listener, and enough chances to try.”

— My teaching philosophy · 因材施教

A few practical questions

Before your first lesson.

Still wondering something? Ask in your inquiry — I’m happy to answer before you book.

How do online lessons work?

We meet one-to-one on Zoom or Google Meet for 55 minutes. I share a screen with your lesson materials, and you’ll receive notes, corrections and a small homework task afterwards. All you need is a stable connection, a microphone and somewhere reasonably quiet.

I’m a complete beginner. Will I feel lost?

Not here. We start with pinyin and tones at a pace that works for your ear, and I explain everything in clear English until you’re ready for more Chinese. Many of my students started with “nǐ hǎo” and nothing else.

Which time zones can you teach in?

I’m based in Shanghai (GMT+8) and teach learners across Europe, North America, Asia and Australia. I keep early-morning and evening slots for different time zones. Tell me your availability in the form, and I’ll suggest a few options.

Can I reschedule or cancel a lesson?

Yes — please give at least 24 hours’ notice and we’ll move the lesson at no cost. Later cancellations count as a used lesson, though I’ll always try to be flexible when life happens.

Do you teach children?

I teach young learners from around age seven, as well as teenagers and heritage speakers. For children, I ask a parent or guardian to join the first five minutes of the intro call so we can align on goals and lesson format.
