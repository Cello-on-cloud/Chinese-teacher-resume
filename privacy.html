Who I am 关于我

Zhixia Chinese (“the site”, “I”, “me”) is the personal Chinese-teaching website of [your full legal name or registered business name], based in [your city, country]. I am responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

For privacy questions or requests, email hello@zhixiachinese.com.

What I collect 我收集哪些信息

I try to collect only what I need to reply to you and teach well. Depending on how you interact with the site, this may include:

Information you give me

  • Your name and email address.
  • Optional details such as nationality, native language, Chinese level, learning goals, preferred schedule, time zone, WeChat or WhatsApp handle, and any notes you choose to share.
  • Your selected lesson package and preferred start date.
  • Messages you send by email or through the inquiry form.

Information created when you book or pay

  • Lesson bookings, attendance and scheduling notes.
  • Payment status, amount, currency, order reference and the payment method you selected. Card and bank details are handled by the payment provider — I do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Correspondence about rescheduling, refunds or support.

Technical information

My hosting and security providers may process standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, approximate location, referring page and access times in server logs when you visit the site. The site also loads Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address. See Cookies & fonts.

How I use it 我如何使用信息

I use your information to:

  • Reply to your inquiry and recommend a suitable lesson plan.
  • Arrange, deliver and reschedule lessons.
  • Prepare materials, keep lesson notes and follow your progress.
  • Process payments, send receipts and manage refunds.
  • Keep basic business and tax records.
  • Protect the site, prevent spam or misuse, and comply with legal obligations.

Where data-protection law uses “legal bases”, I rely on: your consent (for example, when you submit an inquiry), performance of a contract (when I provide lessons you have booked), legal obligations (such as tax records), and legitimate interests (such as replying to messages and keeping the site secure). I do not use your information for automated decision-making or sell it.

When I share it 何时共享信息

I do not sell or rent your personal information. I share it only when needed to run the teaching service, with providers such as:

  • Payment processors (for example, Stripe or PayPal) to take payments and handle refunds.
  • Email and scheduling providers used to reply to inquiries and send lesson confirmations.
  • Website hosting and security providers that keep the site online and protected.
  • Professional advisers or authorities where required by law, or to protect legal rights and safety.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. I choose providers with appropriate safeguards, but I cannot control their independent practices.

International transfers 跨境传输

I teach learners around the world, and the providers I use may process information in countries other than yours. Where required, I rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses, or your explicit consent, for those transfers.

How long I keep it 保留多久

I keep information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for. As a guide:

  • Inquiries that do not become bookings: up to 12 months, then deleted or anonymized.
  • Student and lesson records: for the duration of our lessons and a reasonable period afterwards in case of follow-up questions.
  • Payment and tax records: for the period required by applicable accounting and tax rules.
  • Messages about a dispute or legal claim: until the matter is resolved.

Your rights 你的权利

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal information I hold about you.
  • Ask me to correct inaccurate information.
  • Ask me to delete information that is no longer needed.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Ask for a portable copy of information you provided.
  • Complain to your local data-protection authority.

To exercise a right, email hello@zhixiachinese.com. I may ask for information to confirm your identity, and I will respond within the time required by applicable law. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before the withdrawal.

Cookies & fonts Cookie 与字体

The current build does not set advertising or analytics cookies. The inquiry and checkout demo use your browser’s local or session storage to remember form details on your device; this information is not sent anywhere in the preview.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. That request may share your IP address and browser details with Google. If you add analytics, embedded videos, a booking widget or other third-party tools, update this policy and add an appropriate cookie notice before launch.

Children 未成年人

I teach young learners from around age seven, but lessons for a child must be arranged by a parent or guardian. I do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without parent or guardian consent. If you believe a child has provided information without consent, please contact me so I can delete it.

Security 信息安全

I use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including HTTPS on the live site, access controls and reputable providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so I cannot promise absolute security. Please avoid sending sensitive information such as full card numbers, passwords or identity documents by email.

Changes 政策更新

I may update this policy as the service or the law changes. The latest version will always be on this page, with a new “last updated” date. If a change is material, I will take reasonable steps to let current students know.
