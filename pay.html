Payment & lessons

Good to know.

Simple policies, clear answers. Anything else, just ask before you pay.

How do I schedule lessons after paying?

Your confirmation includes a scheduling link. Choose a time that works in your time zone, and you’ll receive a calendar invite with the Zoom or Google Meet link. Most students book their first lesson within 48 hours.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and cross-border WeChat Pay or Alipay. All prices are in US dollars. Your bank may apply its own currency conversion fee — that is outside my control.

Can I reschedule a lesson?

Yes. Reschedule at least 24 hours before your lesson and there’s no charge. With less notice, the lesson counts as used, though I’ll always try to find a fair solution if something unexpected happens.

What is the fit guarantee?

If after your first lesson you feel we’re not the right fit, email me within 7 days. I’ll refund the cost of all remaining unused lessons in your package — no awkward questions. The first lesson itself is non-refundable.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The price you see is the price you pay, and I cover the payment processing fee. You only need your own internet connection and a free Zoom or Google Meet account. No registration or materials fee.
